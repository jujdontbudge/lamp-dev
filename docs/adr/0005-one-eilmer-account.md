# One Eilmer account across every Eilmer product

People sign in with one Eilmer account, run on Clerk, for Lamp and every other Eilmer product, such as Apple Bridge and future ones. Anything that uses the Eilmer relay requires it. A fleet that doesn't use the relay can also sign people in with local accounts or its own OIDC provider. That way self-hosted and offline fleets keep working when Clerk or the internet is down.
